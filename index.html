Why Camp Turner?

SHADY LAKECalm water, mountain views, boating and swimming!
4 CABINSBeech, Birch, Azalea and Holly.
REMOTE!Far from town, traffic, television and everyday worries.

Camp Turner sits in a quiet hollow between two mountain peaks. Old log buildings surround a central clearing, with paths leading to the lake, dock, dining hall and wooded activity areas.

Staff members arrive several days before the children to clean, repair and stock the camp. Pick the assignment that best matches your talents. Every volunteer is important!
